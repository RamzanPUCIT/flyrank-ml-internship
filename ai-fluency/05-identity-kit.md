# Decide Once: Identity Kit

![logo](identity/logo.svg)

- **Fonts:** headings *Inter* (700), body *Source Serif 4* (400), code *JetBrains Mono*.
- **Palette:** Deep teal `#0F3D3E` (primary) · Saffron `#F2C14E` (accent) · Off-white `#FAF8F3` (background) ·
  Ink `#1D1D1F` (text) · Slate `#6B7280` (muted).
- **Logo / favicon:** `identity/logo.svg` — "MR" monogram, saffron on deep teal, rounded square (works at 16 px).
- **Style note:** Calm, research-paper feel — lots of white space, one accent colour, real charts instead of stock images.
  Plain words first, numbers always next to their baseline.
