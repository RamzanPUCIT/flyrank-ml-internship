# General AI Fluency · Impact Project — Capstone

## Brief (what the project is and why)
**Project:** *Which pages should an editor refresh first?* — a research paper and per-client review queue built
during the FlyRank ML internship, with Claude as my working partner (framing, code review, critique, writing).
- Live paper: https://ramzanpucit.github.io/flyrank-ml-internship/
- Repo + notebooks: https://github.com/RamzanPUCIT/flyrank-ml-internship
- **Purpose:** show I can turn a messy dataset into an honest, ranked decision — and say where the evidence stops.
- **Result:** on client sites never seen in training, a transparent 3-signal rule picked pages that went on to lose
  demand ~81% of the time in its top 50 (vs ~63% by chance); learned models did not beat it, and I reported that.
- AI-fluency pieces: proof statement, stack choice, plain-words explainer, personal-agent design, identity kit
  (all in this `ai-fluency/` folder).
